StoryLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

StoryLens ("the Extension") is a browser extension that helps users highlight and manage glossary terms while reading web novels online. This policy explains what information the Extension handles and how it is used.

Information the Extension processes

1. User-created glossary data
When you use StoryLens, the Extension may create, store, and sync data you enter, including:
• Novel titles and metadata
• Keywords and descriptions
• Categories and natures
• Text replacements
• Colors and display settings
• Uploaded images associated with keywords or novels, if you choose to upload them

This data is sent to the StoryLens API at https://storylens-api.iscoded.com so your glossary can be saved and loaded during future reading sessions.

2. Website content on pages you visit
To highlight and replace terms on a novel page, the Extension reads the visible chapter content on the page you are reading. This processing happens locally in your browser to apply your configured keywords and replacements. If you click Summarize, the Extension copies the active page body, removes scripts and form controls, and sends the remaining HTML through Story Lens Client on your computer to your selected Claude or Codex AI provider. Page content may still contain personal data visible on the page. The summary appears on that same page. This action is optional and never automatic.

3. Account and authentication data
When you first use StoryLens, the Extension may automatically create a guest account with a generated username so your glossary can be saved and synced. If you choose to register or sign in, the Extension may collect and transmit:
• Email address and password (when you register or log in)
• Username and optional display name
• A session token stored locally in your browser for authenticated API requests
• An optional desktop pairing token stored in extension-local storage and sent only to the local client

4. Technical data
Like most web-connected software, requests to the StoryLens API may include standard technical information such as request timestamps and browser/network metadata needed to deliver the service.

Information we do not collect through the Extension
• We do not collect your browsing history across websites
• We do not sell your personal information
• We do not use your data for unrelated advertising profiles

How we use information
We use the information above only to:
• Detect the novel/chapter context on supported reading pages
• Apply your keyword highlights and replacements
• Save, load, and sync your glossary and settings
• Create and maintain guest or registered user sessions
• Summarize the current page when you ask, using your chosen AI provider
• Operate, maintain, and improve StoryLens

Data storage and security
Data synced with StoryLens is transmitted over HTTPS. Data is stored on servers used to operate the StoryLens API. Optional summary requests use loopback HTTP to Story Lens Client on the same computer; the client forwards the page to the selected AI provider via its authenticated CLI. Provider processing and retention follow its own terms and account settings. Story Lens does not store summary requests or outputs on its backend.

Data retention
Your glossary data is retained while you continue to use StoryLens and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your stored data by contacting the developer.

Third-party services
StoryLens uses the StoryLens API hosted at https://storylens-api.iscoded.com. Optional summarization uses the Claude or Codex provider you select. We do not sell user data to third parties.

Your choices
You can stop using the Extension at any time by disabling or removing it from Chrome. Removing the Extension stops further local processing on web pages.

Children's privacy
StoryLens is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
