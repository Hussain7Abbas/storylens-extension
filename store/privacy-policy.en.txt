StoryLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

StoryLens ("the Extension") is a browser extension that helps users highlight and manage glossary terms while reading web novels online. This policy explains what information the Extension handles and how it is used.

Information the Extension processes

1. User-created glossary data
When you use StoryLens, the Extension may create, store, and sync data you enter, including:
• Novel titles and metadata
• Keywords and descriptions
• Categories and natures
• Text replacements
• Colors and display settings
• Uploaded images associated with keywords or novels, if you choose to upload them

This data is sent to the StoryLens API at https://storylens-api.iscoded.com so reference entries can be saved and loaded during future reading sessions. The reference catalogue and your contributions are shared with other authenticated users, rather than kept as a private notebook. Optional image uploads are processed by ImgBB.

2. Website content on pages you visit
To highlight and replace terms on a novel page, the Extension reads the visible chapter content on the page you are reading. This processing happens locally in your browser to apply your configured keywords and replacements. If you click Summarize, the Extension copies the active page body, removes scripts, forms, hidden nodes and controls, and sends the remaining HTML through Story Lens Client on your computer to your selected Claude or Codex AI provider. The same desktop AI path can send selected text and reference context for keyword suggestions, or chapter text and known reference names for keyword extraction, when you request those actions. Page content may still contain personal data visible on the page. These actions are optional and never automatic. If you request AI-assisted chapter-selector detection, the page URL and HTML go to the Story Lens API, then OpenRouter and its model provider.

3. Account and authentication data
When you first use StoryLens, the Extension may automatically create a guest account with a generated username so your glossary can be saved and synced. If you choose to register or sign in, the Extension may collect and transmit:
• Email address and password (when you register or log in)
• Username and optional display name
• A session token stored locally in your browser for authenticated API requests
• An optional desktop pairing token stored in extension-local storage and sent only to the local client

4. Technical data and optional usage analytics
Requests to the StoryLens API may include standard technical information such as request timestamps and browser/network metadata needed to deliver the service. If analytics credentials are included in the Extension build and the General settings switch is on (the default), the Extension sends feature events to Google Analytics 4. These include extension installation and update, screens opened, requested AI actions, and detection of a supported novel page. A novel-page event includes the site's hostname and whether a chapter was detected. Each event includes a randomly generated, persistent analytics client ID, a session ID, and the extension version; events may also include non-content options or counts. We do not send full page URLs, page text, novel or chapter titles, glossary terms, prompts, email addresses, or account IDs in analytics events. Network requests to Google can expose normal connection metadata such as an IP address. Turn off analytics in Settings → General at any time; no further events are sent while it is off. If the build has no analytics credentials, no analytics events are sent.

Information we do not collect through the Extension
• We do not collect a full browsing history or send full page URLs to analytics; when analytics is active, supported reading-site hostnames may be sent with novel-page events
• We do not sell your personal information
• We do not use your data for unrelated advertising profiles

How we use information
We use the information above only to:
• Detect the novel/chapter context on supported reading pages
• Apply your keyword highlights and replacements
• Save, load, and sync your glossary and settings
• Create and maintain guest or registered user sessions
• Summarize the current page when you ask, using your chosen AI provider
• Operate, maintain, and improve StoryLens
• Measure use of reading features when usage analytics is enabled

Data storage and security
Data synced with StoryLens and analytics events are transmitted over HTTPS. Data is stored on servers used to operate the StoryLens API or, for analytics, processed by Google. Optional summary requests use loopback HTTP to Story Lens Client on the same computer; the client forwards the page to the selected AI provider via its authenticated CLI. Provider processing and retention follow its own terms and account settings. Story Lens does not store summary requests or outputs on its backend.

Data retention
Your glossary data is retained while you continue to use StoryLens and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your stored data by contacting the developer.
The analytics client ID remains in extension-local storage until that storage is cleared or the Extension is removed. Google processes analytics data under its own retention settings and privacy policy.

Third-party services
StoryLens uses the StoryLens API hosted at https://storylens-api.iscoded.com. Optional summarization uses the Claude or Codex provider you select; AI-assisted chapter detection uses OpenRouter and its model provider. Optional image uploads use ImgBB. When configured and enabled, usage analytics goes to Google Analytics. See https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google's privacy policy. We do not sell user data to third parties.

Your choices
You can turn off analytics in Settings → General. You can stop using the Extension at any time by disabling or removing it from Chrome. Removing the Extension stops further local processing on web pages; it does not automatically delete your server account or shared contributions.

Children's privacy
StoryLens is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
